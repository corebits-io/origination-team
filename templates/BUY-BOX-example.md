# Buy Box — worked example

> This is a completely fictional buyer, filled in to show what "complete
> enough to run" looks like. Marden Holdings does not exist. Do not copy the
> answers; copy the specificity.

---

## 1. Who is buying

- **Buyer name (firm or person):** Marden Holdings LLC
- **Buying as:** Family holdco, own permanent capital, no fund clock
- **Do you earn a fee for introducing or arranging transactions you do not
  buy yourself?** No
- **Website or one public page that describes the buyer:**
  mardenholdings.com/about

---

## 2. Sectors and segments

- **Sectors in scope:** Industrial services
- **Segments within them (be narrow):** Metal finishing and plating job shops;
  industrial coating applicators (liquid and powder); anodizing. Companies
  that process customer parts for a fee
- **Segments explicitly out:** Captive finishing lines inside OEMs; coating
  manufacturers (we want applicators, not chemistry); architectural/decorative
  coating contractors

---

## 3. Geography

- **Countries:** United States
- **Regions / states / metros in scope:** Ohio, Indiana, Michigan, Kentucky,
  western Pennsylvania
- **Out of scope:** Everything else, including an otherwise perfect company in
  Texas
- **Does HQ location decide, or operating footprint?** HQ decides. We visit
  every plant monthly in year one

---

## 4. Size box

- **Revenue range:** $8M to $40M
- **EBITDA range:** $1.5M to $5M
- **Headcount range (if revenue is unknowable in this segment):** 40 to 220.
  Most of these companies publish nothing; headcount is usually the only
  readable size signal
- **Which figure decides when they conflict?** EBITDA when verified; otherwise
  headcount

---

## 5. Ownership preferences

- **Ownership types in scope:** Founder-owned, family-owned (any generation),
  management-owned
- **Ownership types out:** Sponsor-owned (always), ESOP (we will not unwind
  one), subsidiaries of public companies
- **Does founder-still-active matter?** Preferred. An owner in their 60s with
  no visible successor is our best conversation

---

## 6. Platform or add-on

- **Is this search for a platform, add-ons, or both?** Platform first. After
  one closes, re-run the same box as an add-on search within 300 miles of it
- **If add-ons: name the platform(s) they attach to.** N/A until a platform
  closes
- **What makes an add-on additive here?** New certifications (NADCAP, ISO
  13485 adjacency) or a metro we are not in

---

## 7. Deal preferences

- **Majority or minority:** Majority, 80%+
- **Does management need to stay?** A general manager or strong #2 must exist
  or be hireable; the owner may leave after transition
- **Timeline:** Actively deploying now; one platform in the next 12 months

---

## 8. Exclusions and dealbreakers

- **Hard exclusions:** More than 40% of revenue from one customer (where
  readable); active EPA consent decree; chrome plating as the majority line of
  business; any company currently in a sale process with a banker
- **Soft negatives (lower the rank, never exclude):** Union shop; building is
  leased from a third party; no quality certifications listed anywhere

---

## 9. Outreach identity

- **Who signs the outreach (name, role):** Tom Marden, Managing Partner
- **One sentence on why this buyer is credible to an owner:** Second-generation
  family holdco that has owned and operated one plating business in Columbus
  for 19 years and has never sold a company it bought
- **Channels you will actually use, by hand:** Posted letter to the registered
  office first; LinkedIn second; phone only after a reply
- **Anything you refuse to say in outreach:** Never "we have buyers for
  businesses like yours". Never a valuation number before a conversation.
  Never urgency

---

## 10. Capacity

- **How many owner conversations a month can you genuinely take?** 6
- **How many first touches a week will you send, by hand?** 10
