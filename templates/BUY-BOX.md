# Buy Box

Fill in every section. Write `TBD` where you genuinely do not know and the
intake skill will help you decide. Write `N/A` where a section truly does not
apply. Do not leave blanks: a blank reads as "skipped", and skipped sections
become unexamined criteria that exclude real companies silently.

The worked example in `templates/BUY-BOX-example.md` shows what a usable box
looks like.

---

- **Ownership types in scope:** (founder-owned / family-owned /

## 9. Outreach identity

The outreach-prep skill reads this. Drafts are written in this voice and
signed by this person. If this section is empty, drafting stops.

- **Who signs the outreach (name, role):**
- **One sentence on why this buyer is credible to an owner:**
- **Channels you will actually use, by hand:** (posted letter / email you

---

## 10. Capacity

- **How many owner conversations a month can you genuinely take?**
- **How many first touches a week will you send, by hand?**

The ranking skill uses this to size the PRIORITY bucket. A PRIORITY list
bigger than your calendar is a WATCH list wearing a costume.
